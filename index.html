How to play

  • Clear lines to score. Quads, T-spins, combos and back-to-backs build HEAT.
  • Heat multiplies everything you earn (+30% per star) and drops pieces faster.
  • At 3★ the pursuit starts: a roadblock row rises when the meter fills.
  • At 5★ a GETAWAY begins. Clear 6 lines in 25s to escape with a big bonus. Miss it and two roadblocks rise.
  • $ blocks are cash. Clear their row to grab it. Spend cash on new districts.
  • Go quiet for 8s and the heat cools off. Or bribe the cops with run cash to drop a star.
  • At 4★ a chopper searchlight sweeps the board. Keep your piece out of the light or the pursuit fills faster.
  • Contacts call with jobs. Finish them in time for cash and respect, which raises your rank.
